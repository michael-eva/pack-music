Strip the hero Stanley embellishments and make them larger

## Problem
- The hero Stanleys on the top of each page currently have added effects (white outline or sticker cutout) that the user did not ask for.
- They are also too small compared to the heading text.

## Goal
- Remove all outlines, filters, and cutout effects from the hero Stanleys.
- Keep them as plain GIFs.
- Increase their size so they balance the headline.

## What to change
- `src/components/site/blocks.tsx`:
  - Remove `dark` and the conditional styling from `PageHero`’s Stanley.
  - Bump the hero Stanley classes from `h-40 w-40 sm:h-52 sm:w-52 lg:h-60 lg:w-60` to `h-52 w-52 sm:h-64 sm:w-64 lg:h-80 lg:w-80` (or similar) so it sits roughly level with the headline.
  - Leave the `Stanley` component itself and its `dark` prop unchanged for other uses (e.g. light zine cards).
- Verify the homepage is still intentionally Stanley-free.
- Preview the `/artists`, `/listeners`, `/partners`, `/about`, and `/join` pages for balance.

## Out of scope
- No changes to the footer running Stanley.
- No new colours, textures, or global layout changes.
